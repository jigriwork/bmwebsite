Our Story

Brand Mark is a premium multi-brand fashion store based in Berhampur, built on years of trust, experience, and understanding of what customers truly want.

Our roots go back to a family business that started even before India’s independence. Over the years, we have grown step by step, building strong relationships and a reputation for quality and reliability.

In 2007, we expanded with Go Planet, a large-format fashion store serving a wide range of customers. With that experience and trust, Brand Mark was created as a dedicated premium space for those looking for better brands, better styling, and a better overall shopping experience.

At Brand Mark, we bring together some of the most trusted and popular fashion brands for men and women. Our focus is simple: good quality, latest styles, and a store experience that feels comfortable and well-organized.

We also proudly offer our own label, MITTY, where we focus on creating premium products with modern designs and strong attention to quality.

Today, Brand Mark continues to grow as a trusted name in Berhampur for premium fashion.
